Most companies need AI leadership **before** they can justify a full-time Chief AI
Officer. That is where the fractional role fits: senior, part-time leadership,
accountable for turning AI into a prioritised, governed business agenda.

## What it does

- Translates business objectives into a portfolio of initiatives prioritised by impact and effort.
- Governs risks, decisions and internal sponsorship.
- Maintains an executive cadence: AI committee, quarterly roadmap, value dashboard.

## What it does not do

- It is not a tool vendor arriving with an answer before understanding the question.
- It does not replace teams: it directs and enables them.
- It does not promise savings without a baseline or validation.
