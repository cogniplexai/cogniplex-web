Many companies have already tried AI. They have opened ChatGPT, set up some automation, perhaps
commissioned a chatbot. And yet the way the company operates has barely changed. The reason
is simple: **accumulating scattered AI experiments is not the same as transforming a company.**

## The mirage of scattered pilots

A pilot — a bounded experiment to see whether something works — proves that an idea is
possible. But a capability only creates value when it is embedded in a real process, the right
people use it with confidence and there is a way to measure its impact. Without that, the pilot is
abandoned and the knowledge evaporates.

## What actually transforms

An AI-augmented company is one in which intelligence measurably extends people's
capability: it speeds up decisions, reduces repetitive work, protects knowledge and
enables growth without an equivalent escalation in costs.

That is not achieved with a tool, but by connecting five layers: **strategy, data,
technology, processes and people.** That is the work.

> AI is not the product. Business transformation is the product. AI is the means.
